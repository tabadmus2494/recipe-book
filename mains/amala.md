# 🍲 How to Make `Amala`

Amala is a soft swallow made from yam flour (*elubo*). It is traditionally enjoyed with **gbegiri** (bean soup) and **ewedu**, often combined as the famous Yoruba ethnic group dish **Abula** in Nigeria.

---

## 🌾 Ingredients
- 1 cup **yam flour (elubo)**  
- 2–3 cups **water**

*(That’s all you need – no salt or oil.)*

---

## 🍳 Method

1. **Boil the water**  
   - Put 2–3 cups of water in a pot.  
   - Allow it to come to a rolling boil.  

2. **Add yam flour gradually**  
   - Reduce the heat slightly.  
   - Sprinkle the yam flour (*elubo*) into the hot water little by little.  
   - As you pour, stir continuously with a wooden spatula (*omorogun*) to avoid lumps.  

3. **Stir until smooth**  
   - Keep turning and pressing it against the sides of the pot until it forms a smooth, stretchy dough.  
   - If too thick, add a splash of hot water, cover, and let it steam for 1–2 minutes, then stir again.  
   - If too soft, sprinkle in a little more yam flour and stir well.  

4. **Steam briefly**  
   - Add a few tablespoons of hot water on top, cover the pot, and allow to steam on very low heat for 1–2 minutes.  
   - Stir one final time until smooth and stretchy.  

5. **Serve**  
   - Shape into balls with a spatula or wrap in cling film.  
   - Serve hot with **gbegiri soup** (and optionally *ewedu*).  

---

## ✨ `Tips`:
- Work fast when stirring to prevent lumps.  
- Use a strong wooden spatula for easier turning.  
- Perfect amala should be **soft, stretchy, and lump-free**.
